Program Details
The 10% Leaseback Glow Program — the ownership advantage
  • Lease your residence back for the first two (2) years — a secure, predictable, fully hands-off investment.
  • Guaranteed 10% return per year based on the unit's gross purchase price — paid regardless of occupancy, seasonality, or market performance.
  • Owners remain responsible only for property taxes, HOA assessments, required insurance, FF&E reserve, and electricity.
  • Quarterly payments provide simple, reliable cash flow.
  • Participation requires signing the Rental Management Agreement.
  • After Year 2, the unit transitions to the Marriott Bonvoy Rental Program.
